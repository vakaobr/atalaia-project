# Code of Conduct

Atalaia brings together players, field owners, makers and developers from different countries and backgrounds. We want this to be a friendly, welcoming place for all of them.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Keep the field spirit: play fair, call your hits, help others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Promoting real violence or the misuse of game equipment.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, the community hub, and any event or channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban participants who break this code.
