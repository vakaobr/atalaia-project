**Checking in at the field: your data**

Who handles your data: **{operator}**, who runs the game at this field.

What is kept when you check in: the name or callsign you type, your team preference, the replicas you declare for this game, your association and membership number and its expiry if the field asks for them, and the time you checked in. Nothing verifies them with anyone: a marshal compares your physical membership card with what you typed, because the field has to check it (a legal duty for fields in Portugal, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR; the field's legitimate interest elsewhere, Art. 6(1)(f)). Once the card is confirmed, the number is erased from the controller; the association, the expiry date, the result (for example "valid on 2027-01-15") and who checked it stay for the period below. Your phone makes an id for this field; the field keeps that id and a hash of the secret that goes with it. No email, phone number, date of birth, identity document or photo is asked for.

Your position: only if you tick the separate box "Share my position with the game during this game" when you check in (it is unticked, and saying no changes nothing else about your check-in or the game). Your squad or team as the game mode allows, the marshals and the game master see it; the remote game master only if the field turns that on, and it is not stored there. You can stop sharing at any time during the game. We record your choice with the time and this notice's version. The field's controller keeps only the latest position and deletes it when you stop or the game ends.

What for: running the game and keeping replicas safe (the chronograph). The game's events, the scoreboard and whoever watches them get only your id, never your name.

Who sees it: the field's staff. If this field's controller is connected to a site server, your id, the game's events and your chronograph readings are sent to it; your name never is, and your membership number is erased before then.

For how long: your name, team preference, declared replicas, and your membership number if no card check erased it earlier, are erased {retention_days} days after the game, or at once if you leave the game in the app. Chronograph readings are kept for {chrono_retention_days} days. The game's events, scores and the list of its devices are kept for {game_retention_days} days after the game ends. The field id is deleted after a year without a check-in.

Your rights: you can see your data in the app; you can leave the game and erase it in the app, and ask the field's organisers to erase or correct anything they hold about you. Complaints: the CNPD, www.cnpd.pt.
