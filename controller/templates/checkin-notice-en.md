**Checking in at the field: your data**

Who handles your data: **{operator}**, who runs the game at this field.

What is kept when you check in: the name or callsign you type, your federation number if you give it (nothing checks it automatically), the team you prefer, the replicas you declare for this game and the time you checked in. Your phone makes an id for this field; the field keeps that id and a hash of the secret that goes with it. No email, phone number, date of birth, identity document or photo is asked for.

What for: running the game and keeping replicas safe (the chronograph). The game's events, the scoreboard and whoever watches them get only your id, never your name.

For how long: your name, federation number, team preference and declared replicas are erased {retention_days} days after the game. The field id is deleted after a year without a check-in. Chrono readings follow the field's own retention rules.

Your rights: you can see your data in the app; you can leave the game and erase it in the app, and ask the field's organisers to erase everything they hold about you. The data stays on the field's controller and does not go to the internet.
