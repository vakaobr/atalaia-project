**Checking in at the field: your data**

Who handles your data: **{operator}**, who runs the game at this field.

What is kept when you check in: the name or callsign you type, your team preference, the replicas you declare for this game, your membership number and its expiry if the field asks for them (nothing checks them with anyone), and the time you checked in. Your phone makes an id for this field; the field keeps that id and a hash of the secret that goes with it. No email, phone number, date of birth, identity document or photo is asked for.

Your position: only if you choose to share it, during a game. The field's controller keeps only the latest one and deletes it when the game ends. If the field has a remote game master, they see it live too; it is not stored.

What for: running the game and keeping replicas safe (the chronograph). The game's events, the scoreboard and whoever watches them get only your id, never your name.

Who sees it: the field's staff. If this field's controller is connected to a site server, your id, the game's events and your chronograph readings are sent to it; your name and membership number never are.

For how long: your name, membership number, team preference and declared replicas are erased {retention_days} days after the game, or at once if you leave the game in the app. Chronograph readings are kept for {chrono_retention_days} days. The game's events, scores and the list of its devices are kept for {game_retention_days} days after the game ends. The field id is deleted after a year without a check-in.

Your rights: you can see your data in the app; you can leave the game and erase it in the app, and ask the field's organisers to erase or correct anything they hold about you. Complaints: the CNPD, www.cnpd.pt.
