Built into site/montagem/, site/en/build-guides/, site/de/bauen/ and site/zh-tw/build-guides/. Increment 8 of the draft RFC "The expansion port" built the target module's firmware, its pin table, its UF2 files and the box reading the port; what still needs real hardware (the module's current and 3V3, the sensor's tuning and divider, the port's tests with a module, Target Windows as a preset) is listed "After the bench" (build.after_bench), never invented (decisions EXPI10-9, SITEB-1 to SITEB-9, SITE8-1 to SITE8-9). The target module's pin table holds the RP2040's GPIO numbers of firmware/target-module/src/main.c (EXPI8-9); they have no Kconfig symbol, so check_wiring.py does not check them: change them there, here and in hardware/target-addon/BUILD.md and BUILD.pt.md together. The I2C output module's section (increment 11, SITE8-10 to SITE8-12) holds the pins of firmware/output-module/src/board.h in the same way; tools/check_wiring.py checks them in hardware/output-module/BUILD.md and BUILD.pt.md, not here. The Field Box's wiring drawings (the two SVGs of hardware/field-box/wiring/) are copied into site/img/wiring/ by tools/build_site.py (WIRING there), with --check; their labels stay in English (see site/README.md). The pin tables are the same in every language, so their GPIO numbers live here, not in the language files: each GPIO cell names its Kconfig symbol in data-kconfig, and tools/check_wiring.py (in tools/check.sh) fails if a number is not that symbol's default in firmware/esp32/main/Kconfig.projbuild. The photo placeholders (class "photo") of the Satellite, the pod, the port and the target stay until their first builds' photos (plan phase HW2); never a mock-up or generated photo. The Field Box already has its first real photos and clip (the bench build of 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01, #field-box-first-build). See site/README.md. #} {% include head %} {% include header %}
