{# The screenshot viewer. The script below opens a screenshot's link in this dialog: the image as large as the screen allows (at most three times its size, in whole steps when it is small, so its pixels stay sharp), its alt text as the caption, previous and next (also the arrow keys), closed with Esc, the close button or a click outside the image; focus goes back to the screenshot. It is inline, loading nothing. Without it, or in a browser without , the links open the images. Keep "{" and "}" in the script apart: two together are a placeholder. #}
