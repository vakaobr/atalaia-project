# Privacy notice: Atalaia preview server

Version preview-2026-10-30. This notice covers the preview at **tests.atalaia-project.link** and the live demo
at **demo.atalaia-project.link**. It does not cover airsoft fields that run Atalaia: each field has its own notice.

## 1. Who is responsible

The preview is run by the Atalaia project's maintainer, **Anderson Leite**, a private individual in Portugal,
who is the data controller. Contact for anything about your data: **falecom@andersonleite.me**.

## 2. What the preview is

A place to try Atalaia and say what you think. **The games, fields and players you see are invented** and are
reset from time to time. It is not for real games: please do not enter real players' data.

## 3. What we keep, why, and on what basis

| Data | Why | Basis (GDPR Art. 6(1)) | Kept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Feedback** you send: the kind, the text, optionally who you are in a word, the page you came from, the language | To improve Atalaia | (a) your consent: you choose to send it | 12 months |
| The **contact** you may add to your feedback (an email or other) | To answer you | (a) your consent | 90 days, then erased |
| **Account**, if you create one: username, optional email, a password hash (never the password), two-factor secret (encrypted), passkeys' public parts, roles | To let you try the staff and player pages | (a) your consent, recorded when you register, with this notice's version | Until you erase it (your account page) |
| **Google or Microsoft sign-in**, if you choose it: only the provider's name and the opaque identifier it gives for you | To log in without a password | (a) your consent | Until you unlink it or erase the account |
| **Sessions**: a hash of the login token, the IP address and browser of each login | To keep you logged in and the account safe | (f) security of the service | 30 days after the session ends |
| **Security log**: logins, failed logins, lockouts, account changes, with the IP address | Security and abuse prevention | (f) security of the service | 36 months; your addresses are removed if you erase your account |
| Your **IP address** when you send feedback | To limit messages per address | (f) preventing abuse | In memory only, never stored |

Nothing is used for advertising or profiling, sold or shared, and no decision about you is automated.
The **live demo** (demo.atalaia-project.link) is read-only: it keeps nothing about its visitors.

## 4. Who else sees data

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** carries the traffic of both sites (it ends the encryption and passes the requests on), so it
  sees IP addresses and the requests. It acts as a processor. It is a US company certified under the EU-US Data
  Privacy Framework (Commission Decision 2023/1795).
- **Google or Microsoft**, only if you choose to sign in with them: your browser goes to them, and they learn
  that you are signing in here. They are separate controllers with their own privacy policies.
- The server itself runs on the maintainer's own computer in Portugal. No one else has access.

## 5. Cookies and storage

Only what the sites need to work: a cookie that keeps you logged in (only after you log in), a cookie for the
language you choose, the staff pages' login token in your browser's session storage, and the security cookies
Cloudflare may set against bots. No analytics, no advertising, nothing from other sites.

## 6. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, erase or receive your data, object to processing based on (f), and withdraw a
consent at any time (this does not affect what was done before). Your account page lets you see and erase your
account yourself; for feedback, write to the contact above (say what you wrote and when). You may also complain
to the **Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)**, www.cnpd.pt.

## 7. Age

The preview is for adults trying the software. If you are under 16, please do not create an account or leave a
contact.

## 8. Changes

A new version of this notice gets a new version name; if you have an account, you are asked to agree again at
your next login.
